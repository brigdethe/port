Producer Tag /prəˈdjuːsə tæɡ/ A producer tag is a short audio phrase — usually 3 to 8 seconds long — that says the name, brand, or slogan of a music producer, DJ, or videographer. Tags help protect your work and lets listeners know who created it. Voice tags can be used DRY or with FX, depending on your style. Also known as:Voice TagBeat TagAudio DropDJ Drop

Voice tags are typically short phrases, often ranging from 1 to 5 seconds, designed to be concise and impactful. ( E.g. “Oh my God, Ronny!” )

Yes! Feel free to use your voice tag in any form you wish. Voice Tags do not meet the minimal creative standard for copyright protection, however, they can be trademarked under your name or that of your business.

DRY ( RAW ) signal refers to an unprocessed audio file without any additional processing or effects applied. WET signal, on the other hand, refers to the same audio file but processed and with effects applied.
